The Bible text and the explanations

Every verse in the app is from the World English Bible (WEB), a modern English translation in the public domain. The text is taken word for word from the published edition; we leave out only the headings of the Psalms and a closing “Selah”. We never rewrite or paraphrase it.

The short explanations in “In context” are brief summaries, written by us, of what named public-domain commentaries say about the verse, such as Matthew Henry’s Concise Commentary, Barnes’ Notes, the Cambridge Bible, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown, Ellicott’s Commentary and the Pulpit Commentary. The sources are named with each explanation. The topics a verse is filed under are also our own editorial choice.

The explanations are a help for seeing a verse in its setting. They are not a replacement for reading the whole passage, for the teaching of your church, or for the counsel of a pastor. The app is also not medical, psychological or other professional advice. If you are in crisis, please contact someone you trust or your local emergency number.

We take great care with the text and the explanations. If you find a mistake, please tell us at activley@proton.me so we can correct it.
